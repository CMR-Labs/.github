# CMR Labs

**Research, Engineering & Innovation — CMR Group**

---

## What is CMR Labs?

CMR Labs is the research and engineering arm of CMR Group. It serves as the company's innovation laboratory — responsible for AI experimentation, prototype development, open-source initiatives, software engineering, and the creation of core technologies that power future CMR products.

---

## Relationship to CMR Group

CMR Group is a technology and intelligence-driven company building AI-powered digital infrastructure, intelligent operational platforms, and automation systems for businesses, institutions, and public-sector organizations across Africa.

CMR Labs is where the ideas start. Products and platforms built here eventually graduate into full CMR Group offerings.

---

## Research Areas

- **AI & Automation** — Applied AI research using large language models, NLP, and workflow automation to build intelligent operational systems
- **Systems Design** — Architecture and engineering of scalable, production-grade platforms built for real institutional deployment
- **Criminology & Technology** — Intersection of security studies and software engineering, exploring how technology can address crime monitoring and public safety challenges
